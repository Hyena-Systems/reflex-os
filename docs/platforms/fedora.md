\# Fedora Platform



\## Purpose



Fedora is currently the primary development and experimentation platform for ReflexOS.



At this stage of development, Fedora serves as the underlying operating system used for:



\- Development

\- Testing

\- Audio experimentation

\- Accessibility evaluation

\- Build environment research



\## Why Fedora?



Reasons include:



\- Strong Linux ecosystem

\- PipeWire support

\- Modern package availability

\- Open source foundation

\- Accessibility testing opportunities



\## Current Status



ReflexOS is not yet a standalone operating system.



At present, ReflexOS is being researched and prototyped on top of Fedora while architecture, accessibility systems, audio systems, and development workflows continue to evolve.



\## Future



As ReflexOS grows, the relationship between Fedora and ReflexOS may evolve.



Current development assumes Fedora as the primary development environment.
